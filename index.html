  • SEMA / Finance · Transactional analysis & validation

    Accounts Receivable Aging Reporting

    A positive order balance can hide a different picture at the line level. I work through bundles, payment rollups, and balance exceptions to develop and validate usable aging reports for Finance.

    • Salesforce
    • Nimble AMS
    • Reporting
    • Reconciliation
    • Validation
    Read case study: Accounts Receivable Aging Reporting
    Challenge
    Order-level balances do not tell the whole story of outstanding receivables. Bundled transactions, payment rollups, and positive overall balances with negative underlying lines complicate what should appear in an aging report.
    My contribution
    I develop and validate receivables reporting against Salesforce/Nimble AMS transactional data, translating Finance’s needs into reporting logic.
    Approach
    Work from the financial question back through Order Item and Order Item Line relationships. Reconcile order and line balances, examine bundle behavior and payment rollups, and validate how reporting exceptions affect the outstanding receivables shown.
    Result
    Finance’s requirements are being translated into explicit reporting logic grounded in the transactional relationships. Development and validation continue as balance and rollup exceptions are worked through.
