CRM Relationship & Business Rule Integrity

Investigated recurring CRM relationship discrepancies across connected systems and built repeatable tracking for new failures.

Used SQL/SOQL and record-level comparisons to isolate affected patterns, monitor failures over time, and investigate potential automated remediation. Some failures traced to underlying relationship configuration.
Solution
Created a repeatable tracking and monitoring process for newly occurring failures and documented verified findings for root-cause correction.
Impact
Helped shift the response from isolated tickets toward configuration correction and recurrence prevention.

Payment Reminder Process & Data Quality

Reverse-engineered the audience-building process and helped prevent inaccurate payment communications before deployment.

Problem
The audience depended on manually combined reports and spreadsheet lookups, with different invoice categories requiring different handling.
Investigation
Traced reporting and filtering limitations, clarified business rules with Finance, Sales/Operations, and related stakeholders, and identified questionable recipient and payment data.
Solution
Helped pause and rebuild the audience, validate checkout/payment links, and refresh data near deployment to account for recent payments.
Impact
The campaign was paused and rebuilt before inaccurate communications were sent, reducing the risk of stale reminders reaching recently paid customers.
