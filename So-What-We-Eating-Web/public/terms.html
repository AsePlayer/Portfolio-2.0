Terms of use

Updated October 17, 2026.

So What We Eating? chooses a restaurant and an itemized order within your party budget. Google Maps supplies restaurant discovery and venue information. AllMenus supplies exact listed menu prices only when we can verify the same physical restaurant. Google does not supply or verify those menu prices. Use of Google-derived information is also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.

Published menus can be out of date. Actual prices, availability, taxes and tips may differ. The separately reserved budget buffer is a planning allowance, not an invented tax or tip, checkout quote or guarantee. Retrieval time records when we read the menu, not when the restaurant last updated it. Item prices exclude tax and tip. Confirm current details before traveling or ordering.

Searches consider a bounded sample, not every nearby restaurant. The selected distance is a straight-line radius, not a driving route. Some real restaurants have no trustworthy priced menu we can use. A location or source-service failure never substitutes fictional restaurants. The site owner must configure Google discovery; visitors do not need an account or API key.

Opening-hours filtering is suspended; the app makes no promise of current availability and does not verify dietary or allergen safety. Explicit demo mode contains fictional restaurants and prices; do not travel to sample addresses.

Use this app for personal meal decisions. Do not automate bulk collection, bypass source restrictions or rate limits, or build a permanent provider-content database. The app is provided as available, to the extent permitted by applicable law. See the Privacy policy for location handling and local records. Contact ryanscottcareer@gmail.com.
