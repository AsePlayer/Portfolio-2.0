Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026. This app is operated as part of Ryan Scott’s portfolio.

Location and discovery

We request browser location only after you press “Use my location.” When you search, your coordinates or typed city, ZIP, or address, chosen radius, and Open Now choice go to our Cloudflare Worker and Google Maps Platform to find nearby restaurants. Exact coordinates and typed addresses are not saved in browser settings. Do not enter private information beyond the location needed for your search.

Data on your device

Local browser storage holds budgets, party size, radius, Open Now, adventure and dining preferences, favorite/excluded restaurant IDs, cuisine preferences you choose, and a limited interaction history. A Google-backed completed visit saves a place ID, your action, party size, and timestamp—not the restaurant’s name, address, rating, hours, or coordinates. Display details remain in memory and can be requested again. Fictional demo meals may save their original sample details. There is no account, tracking analytics, or cloud history sync.

Other services

GitHub Pages serves the application. Cloudflare processes network requests and uses IP addresses for short-lived rate limiting. Worker application/request logging is disabled in the supplied configuration. Google processes discovery/geocoding requests under its policies. Provider systems may process service and security data under their own terms. Directions and restaurant website links open external services when you choose them.

The Google Privacy Policy is incorporated into this policy for Google-powered features. See also Cloudflare’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your controls

Decline browser location and type an area instead. Use Preferences → Clear my local data to remove this app’s local records. Browser site-data controls also remove cached shell files and local storage. Offline shell caching never stores live discovery responses. Contact ryanscottcareer@gmail.com with privacy questions.
