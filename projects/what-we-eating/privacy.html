Privacy policy

Updated October 17, 2026. This app is operated as part of Ryan Scott's portfolio.

Location and discovery

We request browser location only after you press “Use my location.” Your chosen coordinates or typed city, ZIP or address go through our Cloudflare discovery Worker to Google Maps Platform. Typed locations use Google Geocoding; coordinates go directly to Google Places. The Worker keeps the Google API key server-side and returns only the restaurant information needed by the app. Discovery responses use no-store. Application logging is disabled; the Worker uses IP-based rate limiting.

Menu prices

Your browser reads AllMenus city listings only for candidate restaurant localities and fetches menus only after a strong branch match. AllMenus receives normal request data including your IP address, but not your typed search or precise user coordinates from this app. Requests omit credentials. Parsed source pages and match results use bounded, temporary memory caches and disappear on reload.

Data on your device

Settings, favorites, exclusions, cuisine choices and interaction history stay in local browser storage. Only “We ate here” saves a completed visit: Google Place ID, party size, time, your selected AllMenus item names and unit prices, and planned total. Google-derived venue names, addresses, coordinates, ratings and cuisines are not persisted. Saved Google visits show their current details only after a fresh lookup. Legacy AllMenus and fictional demo records retain their original provenance. Exact user coordinates, typed searches and resolved Google labels are never saved. There is no account, analytics tracking or cloud history sync.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the app. Cloudflare processes discovery requests, and Google supplies location and venue data under the Google Privacy Policy. See also Cloudflare's privacy policy, AllMenus and GitHub's privacy statement. Directions and source-menu links open external services when chosen. The retained Photon/OpenStreetMap adapters do not run in normal live mode.

Your controls

Decline browser location and type an area instead. Preferences → Clear my local data removes this app's records. Browser site-data controls also remove installed shell files. The service worker caches only the application shell, never external location or menu responses. Contact ryanscottcareer@gmail.com with privacy questions.
