Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026. This app is operated as part of Ryan Scott’s portfolio.

Location and discovery

We request browser location only after you press “Use my location.” When you search, the default free provider sends typed cities, ZIPs, or addresses to Photon and search coordinates/radius to Overpass to find OpenStreetMap restaurants. If Overpass is unavailable, Photon’s restaurant index may also receive those search coordinates/radius. These services receive your network request, including your IP address. Requests and results have a short in-memory cache to reduce repeated public-service traffic; it disappears on reload. Exact coordinates and typed addresses are not saved in browser settings. Do not enter private information beyond the location needed for your search.

Data on your device

Local browser storage holds budgets, party size, radius, adventure and dining preferences, favorite/excluded restaurant IDs, cuisine preferences you choose, and a limited interaction history. A live completed visit saves a provider ID, your action, party size, and timestamp—not the restaurant’s name, address, rating or coordinates. Display details remain in memory and can be requested again. Fictional demo meals may save their original sample details. There is no account, tracking analytics, or cloud history sync.

Other services

GitHub Pages serves the application. Photon and Overpass process free discovery requests. When the optional Google provider is deliberately enabled by the operator, requests instead pass through a Cloudflare Worker to Google Maps Platform. Cloudflare processes those network requests and uses IP addresses for short-lived rate limiting. Worker application/request logging is disabled in the supplied configuration. Google processes discovery/geocoding requests under its policies. Provider systems may process service and security data under their own terms. Directions and restaurant website links open external services when you choose them.

The Google Privacy Policy is incorporated into this policy only for optional Google-powered features. OpenStreetMap processing is described in its privacy policy; Photon is operated by komoot and Overpass by its service operator. See also Cloudflare’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your controls

Decline browser location and type an area instead. Use Preferences → Clear my local data to remove this app’s local records. Browser site-data controls also remove cached shell files and local storage. Offline shell caching never stores live discovery responses. Contact ryanscottcareer@gmail.com with privacy questions.
